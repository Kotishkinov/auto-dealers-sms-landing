Two sources. Either your own CRM — past buyers, service customers and people who already contacted the store — or lists we supply from licensed US data providers, filtered by city, state and profession.

Before anything sends, the list is scrubbed against the National DNC Registry, carrier suppression and your own opt-out history. Traffic goes out on numbers registered under an approved A2P 10DLC campaign, and every message carries dealership identification plus opt-out instructions. We confirm the consent basis for each list with you in writing before the first send — that record is what keeps the campaign defensible under the TCPA and state mini-TCPA statutes.

Four stages, counted separately: delivered, replied, qualified (real buying intent), and test drive booked. Headline conversion is booked appointments divided by messages delivered.

Nothing is modeled or estimated — every number opens to the actual message thread behind it, so you can audit any figure in the report yourself.

You've spent only what was sent — there's no retainer to keep paying and no minimum commitment to unwind.

When a segment underperforms we rewrite the copy, change the send window and re-cut the target list at no additional fee. The 100 free messages exist precisely so you can measure your own market before committing budget.

STOP, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE and the other standard keywords are honored automatically at the carrier level and in our system within seconds. The number moves to a permanent suppression list covering every future campaign for your dealership — it cannot be messaged again, by us or by a later list.

The full opt-out log ships with each campaign report so your compliance file stays complete.

If your store isn't A2P 10DLC registered yet, carrier approval takes roughly 2–5 business days. That's the only real wait. List prep and copy take another day or two, then the first send goes out.

Replies typically start inside the first hour of a send, and the first booked test drives usually land within 48 hours of the campaign going live.
